    Build it in Garmin Connect
    1. In the Garmin Connect app: More › Training & Planning › Workouts › Create Workout › Bike.
    2. Add one step per row above, in order. Set each step's duration in time and its target to Power (custom) or Heart rate (custom) with the range shown. Toggle above between Power and Heart rate.
    3. Save it, then Send to Device. Your Edge picks it up at the next sync, under Training › Workouts.
    Build it for Hammerhead Karoo
    1. Open the Hammerhead Dashboard (dashboard.hammerhead.io) or the Hammerhead Companion app and go to Workouts › New workout.
    2. Add one step per row above, in order, with its duration and the power or heart rate range shown. Toggle above between Power and Heart rate.
    3. Save it. The Karoo syncs it over Wi-Fi or the Companion app; start it from Workouts on the Karoo.
